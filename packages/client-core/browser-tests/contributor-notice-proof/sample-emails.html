The two contributor notice emails, as the Gateway composes them (Team implementation piece 4.4)

Right away: one email for each thing sent

From: DevThrottle
To: the contributor's own account address (read by the website, never by the Gateway)
Subject: Something is waiting for you on DevThrottle

Something new is waiting for you in the team "Erik Laursen".

Open your Inbox to see it.

This email does not say what it is: what was sent to you stays on DevThrottle, where only you can open it once you are signed in.

You get this email because of how you chose to be told. To change it - right away, once a day, or never - open Settings on DevThrottle.
