## v2.25.0 - October 19, 2026

This release fixes empty-composer dictation refusals and intrusive license alarms, adds session renaming on the phone, and improves conversation continuity.

### Highlights
- Dictation: fixes a refusal that treated an empty composer as holding unsent text when its cursor was hidden or parked elsewhere. Genuine unsent drafts, including spaces typed on the prompt row, remain protected from being combined with a new prompt.
- Calmer license status: where license switching is enabled, machines that have stopped reporting, weekly usage limits and accounts with no spare capacity are status on the Licenses page, without alarm banners, push notifications or emails. Existing alarms for those conditions close automatically. Other license faults still raise alarms, but the Cockpit shows only their count and an Open Licenses link in a separate row that does not cover the page. Full explanations stay on Licenses. The phone continues without a license banner.
- Rename sessions on the phone: choose Rename session from a session's menu, edit its name and save. The title updates as soon as the change is accepted. If saving fails, the explanation stays inside the open form.

### Also in this release

These conversation improvements apply to sessions using the optional mode in which the Director controls the conversation directly rather than typing through a terminal:

- Chat opens with the saved conversation and then follows new replies. Saved conversation remains available after a Gateway restart; when older entries have been removed to stay within the history limit, Chat says so.
- Opening Chat waits until the session's connection mode is known. A failed refresh on the phone no longer switches an existing live conversation to a different history view.
- Replies keep their line breaks on the Cockpit and the phone.
- A queued prompt whose outcome was lost when the Director reconnected now says Not known and explains why, rather than staying queued indefinitely or claiming delivery without confirmation.
- The command line shows a queued prompt's place and reason when its receipt is available.
- Session details distinguish Starting, Working and Idle more accurately, including after Hand back.
- Taking the wheel again shows the new terminal from its beginning. Hand back clears the ended terminal, and refused keystrokes show the reason instead of appearing to have been sent.

The hosted Gateway already contains the browser and license changes. Updating the desktop Director installs the dictation and terminal-control fixes, along with the command-line receipt wording.
