## v2.30.0 - October 23, 2026

A session's request to message another session can now be answered from your phone, the Chat opens fast on long conversations, and a factory's checks can read the factory's own records on the Gateway.

### Highlights
- Answer message requests from your phone: when a session asks for permission to message another session, your phone gets one notification and you can approve or refuse it there. The request shows on the Home screen and on its own page, with who is asking, who they want to reach, and the reason in the session's own words. The notification on a locked screen shows only the two session numbers, never the reason or a name. A request you answer at the Director or in the Cockpit disappears from the phone by itself.
- The Chat opens fast on long conversations: the Chat, in the Cockpit and on the phone, now opens on the last ten turns instead of loading the whole conversation. Scroll up, or press Load earlier messages, to bring in the ten before. While it loads it says "Loading the conversation...", and picking another session in the Cockpit clears the previous conversation at once.
- Factory checks can read the factory's records: every document a factory keeps on the Gateway whose name begins with `check-inputs/` is handed to each of that factory's checks as a read-only file in the folder named by `CC_CHECK_INPUTS_DIR`. A factory can now keep the files its checks decide from on the Gateway instead of on one machine's disk. No credential is handed to a check, and a check whose inputs cannot be fetched does not run, so it never decides from nothing.

### Also in this release
- `docs/cli-reference.md` has a new "Factory check inputs" section describing the folder, the size limit and what a check receives.
