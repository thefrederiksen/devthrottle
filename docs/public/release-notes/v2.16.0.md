## v2.16.0 - October 5, 2026

When you correct the Fleet Manager, the correction is kept as a lesson on the Gateway, and every Fleet Manager after it is given that lesson before it acts.

### Highlights
- Fleet Manager lessons. A correction you give the Fleet Manager used to live only in that one conversation, so it was
  lost at the next restart, move or compaction. Now it is kept as a lesson, in your words and with the date, and stored
  on the Gateway rather than on one machine. Every confirmed lesson is given to the Fleet Manager when it starts, when it
  is restarted or moved, when you mark a session as the Fleet Manager by hand, and after a compaction.
- Two ways to keep a lesson. On the Cockpit's Fleet Manager page, press "That was a mistake", type the correction and
  press Keep: it is stored at once, confirmed, and the running Fleet Manager is told at its next free moment. The Fleet
  Manager can also keep one itself with `cc-devthrottle fleet lesson "<your words>"`; a lesson kept that way waits for
  you to confirm it with one press before it is given to a later Fleet Manager.
- Lessons and preferences on one list. The Cockpit's Fleet Manager page shows your lessons first, then your standing
  preferences, each with the date, who kept it and whether it is confirmed. You can confirm, edit and remove them there.
  An account holds at most 20 confirmed lessons of at most 500 characters each; nothing is ever shortened or pushed out.

### Also in this release
- `cc-devthrottle fleet preferences --kind lesson` lists the lessons, and `cc-devthrottle fleet digest` prints them
  first, before the sessions.
- After a compaction the lessons are put back by the session-start hook, on the agents that have one. On the other
  agents the Fleet Manager reads them again with `fleet digest`; the Fleet Manager's agent setting says which agents
  are which.
- When a Director reconnects to the Gateway, it now sends every session's key before it lists any session, so a session
  started while the connection was down can never be claimed by another machine.

The lessons, the Cockpit page and the Fleet Manager's new instructions are in the hosted Gateway. The download carries
the `cc-devthrottle fleet lesson` command and the Director change that puts lessons back after a compaction.
