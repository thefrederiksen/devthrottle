## v2.29.0 - October 21, 2026

Every factory now has a Boss that runs it, Factories come with the Pro and Teams plans, switching to a spare Claude account works on every machine without a hand-edited setting, and a prompt can now be added to a draft you have started.

### Highlights
- Every factory has a Boss: a factory cannot be saved, imported, published or registered without a Boss seat. The Boss publishes changes to its own factory itself, with no trip to the Cockpit; a change to the Boss's own seat still needs your approval, given in your own words.
- Talk to a factory's Boss directly: a Boss you open yourself, to talk with it, now sits inside its factory, with the factory's memory, journals, triggers and schedules. Only the Boss changes when the factory's seats start.
- Factories come with Pro and Teams: on the hosted Gateway, Factories are on for accounts on the Pro or Teams plan. An account without them sees an upgrade page in the Cockpit with a link to Pro and a Try again button, instead of a page that only says Factories are off.
- License switching works on every machine by default: a machine that holds a spare Claude account now switches to it at the limit without anyone editing a settings file. You can still keep a machine out by turning it off with the new `cc-devthrottle license switching off` command, and `status` and `on` do what they say. A machine that holds spares but has switching off now says so on its card on the Licenses page, and a switch records every machine it did not move, so a missed machine can no longer look switched.

- Add to a draft, and let agents press Enter and Escape: a prompt sent to a session whose box already holds text is now typed after that text instead of being refused, so you can start a sentence and let it be finished for you. Agents can press Enter in another session, as the phone's Enter key does, and Escape to clear a stuck session or close a dialog, with the new `cc-devthrottle session enter` and `session escape`. The Fleet Manager may press them in any session; every other session only in sessions it started or owns, and every press is logged. A prompt still waits while you are typing, so your half-finished sentence is never sent with it.

### Also in this release
- A change that widens what a factory's Boss may do - its grants, or the rule sets it names - now needs your approval too, not only a change to its own seat.
- CC Recorder on the phone can share a recording's audio, every part in order, to OneDrive, email or Teams. The Gateway deletes the audio once it is transcribed, so this is the way to get a copy off the phone without a cable.
- The Gateway no longer fails with "unable to delete/modify user-function due to active statements" when two requests open the database at the same moment.
- A Pi session no longer misses something Pi said just before its turn was read.
- You can delete a factory once it is archived. Its schedules and triggers go with it, and DevThrottle tells you which ones were deleted.
- When you set up a Director, your own account is the first choice and the team question is always asked, so a new machine can no longer join a team without you choosing it.
- A Director that is connected to the Gateway after it starts, which is every first run, now gives its sessions their Gateway key again, so those sessions can reach the fleet.
- Mail Desk, a preview that is off by default, can screen a Gmail mailbox as well as Outlook, and acts on the model's answer when the model gives no confidence score.
- `cc-outlook calendar get`, `update`, `delete` and `respond` work again, and `calendar get` shows the event instead of failing.
