## v2.17.0 - October 7, 2026

Closing the installer after a first installation now opens DevThrottle, and signing in works even when Windows opens no browser.

### Highlights
- Closing the installer opens DevThrottle. On a first installation, closing the installer's last page - with the Close
  button or the window's own close button - now opens the Director, exactly as Open Director does. Before, it left
  nothing on screen but a launcher hidden in the tray. A first installation also puts a DevThrottle shortcut on the
  desktop; an update never puts back a shortcut you deleted, and uninstalling removes it.
- DevThrottle comes back after a restart until you have signed in. When you sign in to Windows on a machine that is
  not signed in to DevThrottle, the launcher now opens the Director so its sign-in screen is in front of you. While the
  machine is signed in, it does not do this; if you disconnect it from the Gateway, it starts doing it again.
- Sign-in works when no browser opens. When Windows opens no browser for the sign-in link - a dismissed app chooser,
  or no browser set up at all - the first-run setup and the Gateway connection panel now show the sign-in address with
  Copy and Open in browser buttons, and keep waiting while you finish signing in.

### Also in this release
- Skills: two Directors on one computer no longer remove each other's skills. A Director replaces or removes only the
  skills its own account installed, with one exception: when a personal account and a team both have a skill of the
  same name, the personal account's skill is the one kept.
- Two Directors that share one computer's schedule store no longer both run the same scheduled occurrence; each
  occurrence is claimed by one of them.
- `cc-outlook reply` now goes to the sender only, and `reply --all` to the sender and everyone on the original To and
  Cc. Before, a plain reply went to everyone and `reply --all` crashed.
- `cc-outlook read` works on a draft, and `cc-outlook delete --permanent` now really purges the message instead of
  doing a plain delete.
- The Cockpit: a Cockpit tab opened in the background now loads its session list, instead of showing Loading until
  you look at it.
- Factories, where the factory area is turned on: the Cockpit's Factories screen lists every active factory with what
  is waiting on you, and each factory has its own page with its seats, its activity and reports, and a Talk button
  when it has a CEO. You can mark waiting items as handled, and archive a factory and restore it later from the
  archived list.

The Cockpit and Factories changes are in the hosted Gateway. The download carries the installer, launcher and sign-in
changes, the skills and schedule changes, and the `cc-outlook` fixes.
