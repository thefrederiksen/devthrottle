Item B - rename the factory "CEO" to "Boss"

Your reasoning, as I have it

Not every factory is a company, but every factory has a boss. One word for one idea, no alias: rename and migrate.

What already happened, and what has not

Round 2 of the Factories screen already half-moved away from CEO: the fold says "head" in its own words ("No head named", "Latest from the head", the fallback role "head") and shows each seat's own role from the manifest ("CEO Nora Hale", "CFO Ruth Calder", "Talk to the CEO" when two bosses share a name). So the screen today mixes three words - head, CEO and the role - and the code, the manifest key ceoSeat, the database column CeoSeat, the command line field ceo and the documentation all still say CEO. This rename finishes that move with one word.

The product word

Boss - the seat that runs a factory: decides, hires the other seats, posts the goal number, answers the owner's talk. One per factory, or none. The terminology skill gets this line (edited in its one source, src/CcDirector.Gateway/Skills/Content/terminology.skill.md, the repository copy regenerated, shipped by a Gateway deploy). "Head" and "CEO" are retired as product words; a seat's own role text is the factory's (question 2).

The boss has no name (your ruling of 8 October)

The product never shows a boss by a person's name. A boss is "the boss of Website Business": the Talk button reads "Talk to the boss", the page header reads "Boss", the card reads "Latest from the boss", a talk session is named "Website Business - Boss - talk with the owner", and the Talk seed opens "You are the boss of Website Business". The seat's registered name becomes "Boss" in every manifest that names a boss (eleven), so the data agrees with the screen; the fold's duplicate-name rule ("Talk to the CEO" when two bosses share a name) goes away because there is nothing left to collide. The names collide today - Max Ridley is the boss of two factories and Iris Calloway is a boss and a ClickFunnels seat - which is the confusion you named.

Every other seat is already named by its role (Scout, Sender, Mail Desk, Cost Watch), except four in ClickFunnels: Leo Vance (hacker), Theo Banks (outreach), Iris Calloway (marketing), Nadia Brooks (sales manager). They are listed with ClickFunnels's files below and are not touched here.
